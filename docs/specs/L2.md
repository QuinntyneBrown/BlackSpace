# L2 Requirements — blackspace.ca Responsive Design

---

## L1-1: Hero Section

### L2-1.1: Desktop Hero Layout (≥1024px)
The hero section occupies the full viewport width and height. Headline "Black Canadians in Space & Defence" is large (48–64px), centered. Subheadline sits below at 18–20px. Two buttons horizontally aligned: "Join the Community" (primary, filled) and "Learn More" (secondary, outlined). Background is deep navy/black with subtle gradient or star-field texture.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Desktop frame shows a full-width hero with centered headline, subheadline, and two horizontally-aligned CTA buttons on a dark space-themed background. All content is above the fold at 1440×900.

### L2-1.2: Tablet Hero Layout (768–1023px)
Same content as desktop but headline scales to 36–44px. Buttons remain horizontal. Padding adjusts for narrower viewport.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Tablet (768px) frame shows the hero with appropriately scaled typography and maintained horizontal button layout.

### L2-1.3: Mobile Hero Layout (<768px)
Headline scales to 28–36px. Subheadline to 16px. Buttons stack vertically, full-width. Content remains centered and above the fold on a 375px-wide screen.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Mobile (375px) frame shows stacked buttons, scaled-down text, and all hero content visible without scrolling.

## L1-2: Problem Section

### L2-2.1: Desktop Problem Layout
Section with a heading ("Why This Exists" or similar), 3–4 short paragraphs or bold statement blocks with supporting text. Optional stat callout (e.g., "0 Black Canadian astronauts to date"). Max-width container (~800px) for readability. Dark background with contrasting text.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Desktop frame shows a readable, conversational problem section with bold statements, supporting text, and at least one stat callout, centered within a max-width container.

### L2-2.2: Mobile Problem Layout
Same content, full-width with appropriate padding (16–24px). Font sizes scale down. Stat callout remains visually prominent.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Mobile frame shows the problem section with readable text, proper padding, and a visible stat callout without horizontal overflow.

## L1-3: Who It's For Section

### L2-3.1: Desktop Audience Cards
Section heading + 5 cards in a grid (3+2 or flexible grid). Each card has an icon, role title, and one-line description. Cards have subtle borders or background differentiation.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Desktop frame shows 5 audience cards in a multi-column grid with icons, titles, and one-line descriptions.

### L2-3.2: Mobile Audience Cards
Cards stack vertically, full-width. Each card maintains icon, title, and description.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Mobile frame shows all 5 cards stacked vertically with no horizontal overflow and readable text.

---

## L1-4: What We Do Section

### L2-4.1: Desktop Pillars Layout
Section heading + three equal-width columns for Connect, Share, Elevate. Each column has an icon, pillar name, and 2–3 sentence description.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Desktop frame shows three equal columns with icons, pillar names, and descriptions.

### L2-4.2: Mobile Pillars Layout
Columns stack vertically. Each pillar is a full-width card.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Mobile frame shows all three pillars stacked vertically as full-width cards.

---

## L1-5: Signup / Join Form

### L2-5.1: Desktop Form Layout
Section heading ("Join Black Space Canada"). Form on a slightly lighter panel/card against the dark background. Fields: Full Name (required), Email (required), Current Role/Title, Organization, How did you hear about us? (dropdown). "Join Black Space Canada" button. Reassurance text below.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Desktop frame shows a form card with all 5 fields, a primary CTA button, and reassurance text on a distinct panel.

### L2-5.2: Mobile Form Layout
Form fills the screen width with appropriate padding. Fields stack vertically. Button is full-width.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Mobile frame shows the form with stacked fields, full-width button, and no horizontal overflow.

### L2-5.3: Form Success State
After submission, the form area transforms to show: "You're in. Welcome to the community." with a checkmark icon and a brief message about what to expect next.
**Acceptance Criteria:** A separate frame shows the post-submission success state replacing the form content with a welcome message and checkmark.

## L1-7: Footer

### L2-7.1: Desktop Footer
Dark background. Single row or two rows: contact email, LinkedIn link, disclaimer text, copyright "© 2026 Black Space Canada". Minimal and clean.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Desktop frame shows a dark footer with email, LinkedIn link, disclaimer, and copyright in a clean layout.

### L2-7.2: Mobile Footer
Content stacks vertically, centered. Same elements.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Mobile frame shows footer content stacked and centered.

## L1-8: Navigation & Mobile Menu

### L2-8.1: Desktop Navigation Bar
Fixed/sticky top bar. Left: "Black Space Canada" wordmark. Right: section links (About, What We Do, Join) and a "Join" CTA button. Dark background, translucent or solid.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Desktop frame shows a fixed nav bar with wordmark on left and navigation links + CTA on right.

### L2-8.2: Mobile Navigation Bar
Fixed top bar. Left: wordmark (shortened if needed). Right: hamburger menu icon.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Mobile frame shows a compact nav bar with wordmark and hamburger icon.

### L2-8.3: Mobile Menu Overlay
Full-screen or slide-in overlay when hamburger is tapped. Shows section links vertically with a "Join the Community" CTA. Close (X) button in the top right.
**Acceptance Criteria:** A separate frame shows the mobile menu overlay with vertical links, CTA button, and close button.

## L1-9: Notifications, Alerts, Dialogs & Popups

### L2-9.1: Form Validation Errors
Inline error states on form fields: red border, error message below the field (e.g., "Please enter your name", "Please enter a valid email"). Top-of-form summary banner for multiple errors.
**Acceptance Criteria:** A frame shows the signup form with at least two fields in error state — red borders, inline error messages, and a summary error banner at the top.

### L2-9.2: Success Toast Notification
A toast notification that appears at the top or bottom of the screen confirming form submission: "You're in! Check your email for next steps." Auto-dismisses after a few seconds. Green accent with checkmark icon.
**Acceptance Criteria:** A frame shows a toast notification overlaying the page with success message, checkmark icon, green accent, and dismiss/close button.

### L2-9.3: Already Registered Alert
An inline alert or modal that appears if an email is already registered: "This email is already part of the community. Check your inbox for your welcome email or contact hello@blackspace.ca." Amber/warning accent.
**Acceptance Criteria:** A frame shows an alert dialog or inline banner with the already-registered message, amber/warning styling, and a dismiss action.

### L2-9.4: Cookie / Privacy Notice
A bottom banner or bar informing users about analytics/cookies: "We use analytics to improve your experience. No personal data is sold." with "Accept" and "Learn More" buttons. Non-intrusive.
**Acceptance Criteria:** A frame shows a bottom-anchored cookie banner with message text, Accept button, and Learn More link.

### L2-9.5: Generic Confirmation Dialog
A centered modal dialog with title, body text, and two buttons (Confirm / Cancel). Semi-transparent backdrop. Used for actions like unsubscribe confirmation.
**Acceptance Criteria:** A frame shows a centered modal dialog with backdrop overlay, title, body text, and two action buttons.
